Purchase Rules

  • Only active items can be purchased.
  • Purchase quantity must be greater than zero.
  • Purchase quantity cannot be greater than available stock.
  • The same item cannot be added more than once.
  • Stock is automatically reduced after a successful purchase.
  • Purchases cannot be deleted.
